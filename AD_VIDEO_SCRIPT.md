## 🛠️ Recommended AI Tools to Generate This Video

1. **Voiceover**: [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) (Voice: *Adam* or *Fintech Narrator*, Warm & Crisp Indian/Global English accent).
2. **Video B-Roll**: [Runway Gen-3 Alpha](https://runwayml.com), [Luma Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine), or [Kling AI].
3. **Screen Mockups**: Record quick screen interactions directly from [`https://ccrsk.vercel.app`](https://ccrsk.vercel.app) using Chrome DevTools mobile view.
4. **Final Assembly & Captions**: [CapCut](https://capcut.com) with bold auto-captions (Yellow/Green highlight) and sound effects.
